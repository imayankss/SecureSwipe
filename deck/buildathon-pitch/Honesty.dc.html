Not Razorpay performance

Not a scalability claim

The final scale matrix failed closed, so no multi-worker throughput or production-capacity claim is made anywhere in the repository.

Not a fairness result

SHAP is noncausal, and fairness cannot be evaluated without protected attributes and an authorized study design.
